# Project logo

`logo.png` is the primary logo: the minimal red and black ladybug supplied by
the project owner as `ladybug.png`. The supplied PNG is copied unchanged.

`logo-illustrated.png` preserves the original illustrated concept, created using
the built-in imagegen tool. Its prompt is recorded below.

## Original illustrated logo generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: primary project logo for "Aphids and Ladybugs", a small ecology simulation.
Primary request: a polished bug logo with a green leaf as its background.
Subject and composition: a single coral-red ladybug viewed from above, resting prominently over one broad fresh green leaf. Leaf forms the entire background silhouette, angled gently from lower left stem to upper right tip. Ladybug has a rounded shell, bold near-black spots, center seam, small dark head with ivory cheek accents, six simple short legs and two short antennae. Compose as one cohesive, compact, balanced emblem, centered in a square canvas with comfortable transparent margins.
Style: clean vector-like illustrated logo with smooth confident curves, strong recognizable silhouette, restrained broad cel shading. Friendly but clearly insect-like, refined and simple enough to remain readable at app-icon sizes. Match a nature simulation whose sprites use coral vermilion, chartreuse greens, dark forest outlines and restrained pale highlights.
Leaf: rich fresh green with a simple lighter central vein and only a few broad vein accents; no intricate botanical texture.
Background: genuinely transparent alpha outside the bug-and-leaf emblem.
Constraints: one finished logo only; no words, letters, typography, presentation board, mockup, border, watermark, extra insects, scenery, ground plane or checkerboard. Avoid photorealism, glossy 3D, tiny details and elaborate facial expressions.
